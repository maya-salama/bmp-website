A student mental health club

Caring for our minds, hearts, and iman — together.

The Beautiful Mind Project is a student-run club dedicated to supporting Muslim mental health on campus through education, community, and faith-rooted resources. No one should have to choose between their faith and their mental health — we believe you can honor both.

Who We Are

About Us

The Beautiful Mind Project was founded by students who saw a gap between the mental health resources available on campus and the specific cultural and spiritual needs of Muslim students. We exist to normalize conversations about mental health within our community, connect students to resources that respect their faith, and build a space where struggling is never something to hide.

✦

Education

Workshops, discussions, and blog posts that explore mental health through an Islamic lens — breaking down stigma with knowledge.

✦

Community

Peer support circles and social events where students can show up as their full selves, faith and struggles included.

✦

Resources

A bridge to campus counseling, crisis support, and chaplaincy services — so no one has to navigate it alone.

✦

Advocacy

Working with campus health services to make sure Muslim students' needs are seen and met.
