Crisis & Hotline Support

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Call or text 988 · Available 24/7

Free, confidential crisis support for anyone in emotional distress, nationwide.

Crisis Text Line

Text HOME to 741741 · Available 24/7

Text-based crisis support if calling isn't accessible or comfortable for you.
